**Kütüphaneler**: python-binance>=1.0.19, ccxt>=4.2.0, requests>=2.31.0, beautifulsoup4>=4.12.0, selenium>=4.18.0, web3.py>=6.15.0, numpy>=1.26.0, pandas>=2.2.0, python-dotenv>=1.0.0
